LaneIQ privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026. This is a plain-language policy for an app that keeps your data on your own phone.

The short version

LaneIQ keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive your videos, photos, shots, scores, name or anything else you put in the app.

What is stored, and where

On your phone only (in the app's own storage): your profile (name, the hand you throw with, optional rev rate and the answers to the setup questions), your shots and their measurements, the video clips of your shots, the balls you add and photos' colour signatures used to recognise them, your scorecards, your settings, and a small log of how the lane lock and ball tracking behaved so you can copy it if you need help. It stays there until you delete it. Deleting a profile in the Me tab removes that profile's shots, videos and plan; clearing the website data for the app, or deleting the app, removes everything.

The camera and photos

The camera is used live to find the lane and follow the ball. The pictures are analysed on your phone and are not uploaded. Video clips of your shots are saved on your phone only. Photos you take for "Scan a ball" and the flare-ring photos are analysed on your phone and are not uploaded. The app asks for camera permission, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.

What goes over the internet

  • The app's own files. When you open LaneIQ it loads its code and its ball and oil-pattern lists from its web host (GitHub Pages). Like any website, the host can see your IP address and the time of the request. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The text reader. The first time you use "Scan a ball", a text-reading engine (tesseract.js) and its language data are downloaded from public content networks (jsDelivr and the Tesseract project). Those servers can see your IP address. Your photo is not sent.
  • Sharing with friends. Friend invites and shared sessions are links or messages you send yourself through your phone's share sheet. We do not run a server that receives them. Whatever you choose to put in a link (such as your name and friend code) goes to the people you send it to, through the app you send it with.

Other people on camera

Bowling centres are shared spaces. Point the camera at your own lane and be considerate of other bowlers; follow the centre's rules about filming. Clips stay on your phone unless you choose to share one.

Children

LaneIQ is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because it collects nothing.

Your choices

  • Export your data, or delete a profile, in the Me tab.
  • Turn off the camera in your phone's settings.
  • Clear the app's website data, or delete the app, to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
