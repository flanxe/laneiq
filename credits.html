Ball specifications

Each ball's published specifications (coverstock, core, RG, differential, weights and so on) were gathered from the makers' own public product pages: Storm (with Roto Grip and 900 Global), Motiv, Ebonite, Hammer, Columbia 300, Track, Brunswick, Radical and DV8. Whether a ball is on the USBC approved list comes from the USBC's published list. Where a maker does not publish a figure, other published sources were used: Bowling This Month ball reviews, bowwwl.com and BowlerSmart. Every figure in the app's catalog carries its source address and the date it was read. Specifications can change, and can be wrong at the source: check with the maker or your pro shop before you buy or drill.

Oil patterns

Pattern lengths, volumes and ratios come from the lane-pattern information published by the USBC, and for two patterns from a FloBowling article. Centres and tournaments change patterns: confirm the sheet you are bowling on.

Lane rules

Lane sizes and markings follow the USBC Equipment Specifications and Certifications Manual (January 2024 update).

Coaching targets and layout systems

The skills chart compares you with figures published in Tyrel Rose, "Breaking Down Barriers: Plotting a Course for 220", Bowling This Month, November 2020. Drilling-layout explanations describe the Dual Angle system (Pinel and MoRich), Storm's Pin Buffer system and ball-surface guidance published by Bowling.com. These are described in LaneIQ's own words.

Software

The "Scan a ball" text reader uses tesseract.js (Apache License 2.0) and its language data, loaded from jsDelivr the first time it is used. Reading the body and approach from your clips uses Google's MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (Apache License 2.0), loaded from jsDelivr and Google's model storage the first time it is used. The rest of the app was written for LaneIQ.

Coaching rules

The Move Advisor uses the common coaching rule of moving the feet and the target the same way, the feet about twice as far (the "2-and-1" move). The carry card mentions USBC's ball motion research on entry angle (about 6 degrees at the 17.5 board). The stroker, tweener and cranker rev bands on the Bowler DNA card are the usual rough rule of thumb, not a definition.
